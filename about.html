Romy McCarthy

My name is Romy McCarthy, and I am a senior at the University of Wisconsin-Madison studying Information Science. Outside of school, I am a member of the lightweight rowing team, which has been a big part of my college experience.

I originally took this class mainly because I needed the credits and it counted towards my major, but the class ended up sparking a genuine interest in the relationship between technology, people, and society I have become more interested in thinking about how technology can affect different groups of people in different ways.

I use social media a lot, especially TikTok and Instagram, and I have also been trying to learn more about GitHub and become more comfortable using it. Project 1 made me think more about implicit bias and how people can hold assumptions without necessarily realizing it. It also helped me understand intersectionality as a way of looking at how different parts of someone's identity and experiences can overlap and shape the way they experience the world.

As someone who uses social media every day, I think it is especially important to understand how technology can influence the way people think and interact with each other. I also think learning about bias is important because technology is created and used by people, meaning that the perspectives and assumptions of the people behind technology can affect the experiences of the people using it.

Eddie Maloney

My name is Eddie Maloney, and I am a junior at the University of Wisconsin-Madison studying Information Science with a certificate in Computer Science.

I took this class after taking LIS 444 with Professor Royston, which broadened my knowledge of technology and growth and the racial implications of technological advancement. I wanted to take a deeper dive into the topic, and it is a good fit for my Information Science major.

I am interested in the ways that AI will continue to evolve everyday life. I am especially interested in seeing it come up in more mechanical forms, like physical robots and transportation.

In Project 1, I was surprised that I held certain implicit biases. I learned how many things factor into the way people see the world and how those things influence ourselves and the successes of others.
