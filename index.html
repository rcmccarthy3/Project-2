Welcome

Welcome to our website! Here, we explore the relationship between technology, intersectionality, and implicit bias. Technology is a major part of everyday life, so it is important to think about how people's different identities and experiences can shape the way technology is created, used, and experienced. Throughout our website, you can learn more about these topics, explore helpful resources, and meet a Tech Hero who is working to create a more inclusive and equitable future in technology.

Why This Matters

Understanding intersectionality and implicit bias matters because technology is not separate from the people who create and use it. Our identities and experiences can influence the way we see the world, and those perspectives can affect the technology we built and interact with. This is especially important with social media, artificial intelligence, and other technologies that are becoming part of everyday life. Learning to recognize bias can help us think more critically about the technology we use and consider how it may affect people with different experiences from our own.
